## Forum: new first post for t/1026512

Title: **Floorplan Studio: the floor plan with a real editor (2D, 2.5D, 3D, no YAML)**

> Floorplan Studio draws your home inside Home Assistant and turns it into a
> live dashboard. You draw it in a panel in the sidebar, not in an SVG tool
> and not in YAML.
>
> ![hero](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vespassassina/ha-floorplan-studio/main/docs/img/card-3d.png)
>
> **The editor (Studio)**
> - Walls, rooms, doors, windows, stairs, gardens, terraces, water; snap,
>   typed lengths, undo, an outline and layers.
> - Several floors, aligned on their stairs, shown above each other.
> - Trace over a scan or a photo of your plan.
> - Pick entities from a searchable list; rooms can create Home Assistant
>   areas; design a light scene per room, colours taken from a photo if
>   you like.
> - A demo home to start from: a basement and two floors, furnished.
>
> **The card**
> - 2D, 2.5D and 3D from the same plan. 13 themes.
> - On a phone, a sheet that says what needs attention first (open doors,
>   alarms, lights left on), then what is active.
> - Tap a light for brightness, colour and temperature; covers, climate,
>   media, locks, vacuums, scenes and scripts have their own controls.
> - Motion that fades, a heat map of temperature or humidity, camera
>   thumbnails, night mode at sunset, kiosk mode for wall tablets.
>
> **Install:** HACS, search "Floorplan Studio" (it is in the default list
> now), then add the integration and open Floorplan Studio in the sidebar.
> Home Assistant 2025.6 or later. MIT licence, no cloud, no account.
>
> Repository and docs: https://github.com/vespassassina/ha-floorplan-studio
>
> Bugs and ideas: GitHub issues. Questions and your own plans: here.
> Screenshots of your house are the best feedback I can get.
>
> A new release comes out every Friday; I post what changed below.

## Reddit: r/homeassistant

Title: **I built a floor plan card with a real editor: draw your house in HA, see it live in 2D, 2.5D or 3D**

> Most floor plans in Home Assistant start in Inkscape or Sweet Home 3D and
> end in a page of YAML. I wanted to draw mine inside HA and have it survive
> updates, so I built Floorplan Studio.
>
> - An editor panel in the sidebar: walls, rooms, doors, windows, stairs,
>   several floors, undo. Trace over a scan if you have one.
> - The card shows the plan live in 2D, 2.5D or 3D, with 13 themes.
> - On a phone it opens on a list: what needs attention, what is on.
> - Tap a device for its controls; hold for HA's own dialog.
>
> It is in HACS (search "Floorplan Studio"), MIT, no cloud. There is a demo
> home to load if you want to look before you draw.
>
> [GIF]
>
> Repo: https://github.com/vespassassina/ha-floorplan-studio
>
